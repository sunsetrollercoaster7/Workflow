# Master CV

Put your full master CV here as `master_cv.md`: every role, achievement and skill,
even ones you won't use every time. Each tailored CV is cut down from this file,
so anything missing here can't appear in an application.

Optional extras:
- `profile.md`: contact details, target roles, salary/location preferences, notice period,
  right-to-work status, anything a cover letter might need.
- `cover_letter_notes.md`: tone preferences, phrases you like or hate, a past letter you were happy with.
